Business Name: Brew Bakes

About Us:
Brew Bakes is a small bakery that provides freshly baked cakes, brownies and custom cakes

Products:
We sell birthday cakes, custom cakes, cupcakes, cookies, brownies, pastries and hot chocolate.

Opening Hours:
Monday to Friday: 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
Saturday and Sunday: 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM.

Location:
We are located in Hyderabad, Telangana.

Delivery:
We provide home delivery within Hyderabad.
The standard delivery charge is ₹50.
Same-day delivery is available for selected products.

Payment:
We accept cash, UPI and online payments.

Returns and Refunds:
Custom cakes cannot be returned because they are prepared according to customer requirements.
Packaged bakery products can be returned within 24 hours if there is a quality issue.

Custom Orders:
Customers can place custom cake orders at least 2 days before the required delivery date.

Contact:
Customers can contact  Brew Bakes Bakery during business hours for orders and enquiries.

Special Offers:
Customers may receive discounts during festivals and special occasions.
Current offers should be confirmed with the bakery.
